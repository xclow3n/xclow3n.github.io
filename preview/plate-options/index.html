HAProxy, Netty and nghttpx have none in Simple Icons and none is invented. This is the one landscape plate on the roster; the other eight keep the portrait frame.

What a band prints of a plate is set by the plate’s width, not its height: cover scales to the band’s width, so the slice is the band’s own aspect times the plate width, in the plate’s rows. At 1280×720 that is 422 rows of this plate, and the word and the figures span 732, so both print. The slot number in the corner sits at a fixed fraction of any plate’s width, so the wider frame is also what lets a centred word be 970 wide and void at 163px. The tiles below carry that number at the size and position index.astro gives it.
